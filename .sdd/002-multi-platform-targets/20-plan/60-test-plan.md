## 4. Eval execution and spend

### 4.1 Policy

- **Dev tier** (C001-ADR-003): one run per Examples row, turn cap 30 and spend cap 0.50 USD per
  session (C002-NFR-105, enforced by `evals/harness/caps.mjs` and the runners). No runner checks the
  spend cap before a step (C002-IF-010 §2.2 spend-cap rule). On OpenCode it is checked per step,
  after each step: the step that brings the spend to the cap or above is the last one started and
  counts in full, with any step already running, so a session that ends with `spend cap` records a
  spend at or above its cap by at most the cost of the steps running when the cap is reached, with no
  fixed allowance (C002-NFR-105; 0.511 and 0.525 USD at the 0.50 USD cap and 1.11 USD at the 1.00 USD
  cap in C002-T-056, defect D-022 item 2). The bound is checked after the session from the runner's
  `cap` and `step-after-cap` transcript records (C002-IF-010 §2.2 overshoot rule). On Copilot CLI it
  is checked per session: the cap is the host's `--max-ai-credits` limit, at least 30 AI Credits, and
  the runner compares the session total of the usage file with it after the session (critic CR-64).
  Each result records host, host version, model IDs, tokens, spend, the caps it ran with (`caps`)
  and transcript path under `evals/results/`.
- **Cap exceptions** (C002-IF-010 §2.2 "Caps above 0.50 USD"; P-01 answers to Q-ts634-budget-01,
  "Budget exception $1", and Q-ts634-budget-02, "One run at $2 (Recommended)", 2026-10-20): a case
  row runs with a cap above 0.50 USD only where that table lists the row and host; `run.mjs` ends the
  run before any session otherwise (C002-TC-679). In force: **C002-TS-634 of

## 6. Entry and exit criteria

- **Entry (task)**: dependencies done; the task's fixtures exist; for replay cases the C002-T-042
  driver, for OpenCode eval cases the C002-T-044 runner, for Copilot CLI cases the C002-T-045 runner;
  for a paid OpenRouter session, the OpenRouter precondition of C002-T-001 step 1 and the stop rule

  writes or runs the test, and its exit needs the case to pass. Each further task contributes code,
  fixtures or records the case needs, or runs it again as evidence (C002-T-048, C002-T-049, C002-T-054,
